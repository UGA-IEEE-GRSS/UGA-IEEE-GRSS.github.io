LiDAR Data Processing Seminar

LiDAR is an important remote sensing technology in precision forestry, used to capture highly accurate 3D information about forest structure. It helps measure tree height, canopy density, biomass, terrain features, and forest health with greater speed and accuracy than traditional field methods. In precision forestry, LiDAR supports better decision-making for forest inventory, management planning, conservation, and sustainable resource use.
